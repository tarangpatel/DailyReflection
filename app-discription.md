App Name (Working): Life Intelligence OS

Category: Daily Reflection / Emotional Awareness App

Platform: Mobile-first (iOS + Android)

---

## 🧭 Core Concept

Design a calm, minimalist mobile app that helps users reflect daily and understand emotional patterns over time.

The app acts as a quiet mirror, not a productivity tool.

Users spend ~60 seconds per day logging simple inputs, and receive a weekly narrative reflection generated from their entries.

---

## 🎯 Design Principles

- Calm, slow, and non-addictive
- No gamification (no streaks, badges, or rewards)
- No productivity or performance language
- Emotionally warm and reflective
- Minimal cognitive load
- Feels private and safe

Visual inspiration:

- Soft gradients
- Clean typography (serif + sans-serif mix)
- Gentle transitions
- No harsh colors or sharp contrasts

---

## 👤 Target User

Thoughtful adults (25–45) who:

- Feel mentally busy or emotionally overwhelmed
- Want clarity, not productivity optimization
- Prefer quiet tools over social or noisy apps

---

## 📱 Core Screens to Design

### 1. Onboarding (3–5 screens)

Goal: Feel like entering a calm, personal space

Include:

- Welcome screen (philosophy-driven, not feature-driven)
- “Why are you here?” (short text input)
- Emotional style questions (simple multiple choice)
- Tone preference selection (e.g., calm, poetic, direct)

Design tone:

- Spacious
- Soft transitions
- Minimal UI elements

---

### 2. Daily Check-In Screen (Main Screen)

Goal: Complete in under 60–90 seconds

Elements:

- Mood slider (color-based, not emoji-heavy)
- Energy level slider
- “One word for today” (short input)
- “What mattered today?” (short paragraph input)
- Optional voice note button

Design:

- Single scroll screen
- No clutter
- Focus on input flow
- Keyboard-friendly

---

### 3. Weekly Mirror Screen (Key Differentiator)

Goal: Deliver a reflective, narrative experience

Elements:

- AI-generated weekly summary (150–200 words)
- Subtle visual indicator of mood/energy trend (not chart-heavy)
- Calm reading experience (like a journal page)

Design:

- Full-screen reading mode
- Soft gradient background
- Beautiful typography
- No distracting UI elements

---

### 4. History / Timeline View

Goal: Let users browse past reflections

Elements:

- Weekly cards or timeline
- Each entry shows:
    - Week summary preview
    - Mood color indication

Design:

- Vertical scroll
- Clean and lightweight
- Focus on readability

---

### 5. Settings / Privacy Screen

Goal: Build trust

Include:

- Privacy explanation (simple language)
- Data export option
- Notification toggle

Design:

- Minimal
- Transparent tone

---

## 🎨 Design System Guidelines

### Colors:

- Soft, muted gradients (based on mood)
- Avoid bright, saturated colors

### Typography:

- Serif for reflection text
- Sans-serif for UI elements

### Motion:

- Slow fades
- No bounce or attention-grabbing animations

---

## 🚫 Explicitly Avoid

- Gamification elements (streaks, badges, points)
- Social features (likes, comments, sharing)
- Dashboard-style analytics
- Dense charts or graphs
- Corporate or productivity-style UI

---

## 💡 Overall Feel

The app should feel like:

- A private journal
- A quiet Sunday evening
- A space to pause and reflect

Not like:

- A task manager
- A habit tracker
- A social app

---

## 📦 Deliverables Expected

- Mobile UI mockups for all core screens
- Light and dark mode (optional)
- Consistent design system
- Smooth user flow from onboarding → daily use → weekly reflection

---

End goal: A beautifully designed, emotionally calming app that users return to not out of habit, but because it feels meaningful.
